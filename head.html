Six Passes

Par

      How to play
      1. Start from the left-hand player on the pitch.
      2. Name a club he played for.
      3. Name someone who was at that club in at least one of the same seasons.
      4. Keep passing until you reach the right-hand player.

      Par is the fewest passes the pair can be linked in. A wrong answer is a yellow card. A hint shows one step on the quickest route from where you are.
